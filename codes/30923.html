A2P 10DLC rejection code

A2P error 30923: what it means and how to fix it

Twilio’s name for it: Campaign rejected: Message consent cannot be required for service use. HighLevel’s rejection guide lists the same code.

Twilio: “…your opt-in flow makes consent to receive messages a required condition for using your service…”

twilio.com/docs/api/errors/30923, last updated 27 October 2026, checked 12 November 2026

In plain English

Your sign-up makes texts compulsory. If a customer can’t book, buy or open an account without agreeing to your texts, the consent isn’t voluntary, and the reviewer rejects it. Texting consent tucked inside your terms of service counts as compulsory too.

What usually causes it

  • The booking, checkout or sign-up won’t go through unless the customer agrees to texts.
  • Texting consent is part of your terms of service or another box the customer has to tick.
  • The texting box starts ticked, or there’s no way to say no to texts and still send the form.
  • The message flow doesn’t describe every way people opt in.
  • The opt-in page has no terms or privacy policy links, or the reviewer can’t open it.

How to fix it

  1. Let customers book, buy or sign up without agreeing to texts.
  2. Give texts their own checkbox, apart from the terms box, covering only the messages you’ll send.
  3. Leave it unticked and label it optional.
  4. Where it fits your flow, say that consent isn’t a condition of purchase.
  5. Describe every opt-in method in the message flow, with your website, privacy policy and terms addresses. If the form is behind a login, link screenshots on a public page.
  6. Keep a record of when and how each person agreed, then resubmit the campaign.

Example

Two separate boxes, only the first one required (change the business name and addresses to yours):

[ ] I agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. (required)
[ ] Optional: text me appointment reminders from Your Business. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

Codes that often come with it

  • 30925: Campaign rejected: Opt-in must be unchecked by default; active consent required
  • 30909: Campaign rejected: Message Flow or Call to Action incomplete/unverified

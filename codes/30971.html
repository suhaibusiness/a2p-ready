A2P error 30971: what it means and how to fix it

Twilio’s name for it: Business contact email cannot be verified. HighLevel’s rejection guide lists the same code.

Twilio: “The contact email address cannot be verified as associated with your business.”

twilio.com/docs/api/errors/30971, last updated 27 October 2026, checked 12 November 2026

In plain English

The reviewer couldn’t tie the contact email in your registration to your business. Usually it’s a Gmail, Yahoo or Outlook address that appears nowhere on your website, or an email on a domain that doesn’t match your business name.

What usually causes it

  • You used a free email address (like @gmail.com) and it isn’t shown on your website.
  • The email is on a domain that doesn’t match your business name.
  • Your website lists a different business email from the one in the registration.

How to fix it

  1. Use an email on your own business domain, the same one as your website (name@yourbusiness.com).
  2. If you only have a free address, publish that exact address on your website or official social page so the reviewer can match it, then resubmit.
  3. Make the email in the registration and the one on your website the same address, letter for letter.

Example

Matching contact details (change the business name and addresses to yours):

Website: yourbusiness.com
Contact page shows: office@yourbusiness.com
Registration contact email: office@yourbusiness.com

Codes that often come with it

  • 30915: Sole Proprietor classification is invalid - business is a registered legal entity
  • 30919: Campaign rejected: Website lacks sufficient business or messaging use case info

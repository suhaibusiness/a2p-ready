A2P 10DLC rejection code

A2P error 30556: what it means and how to fix it

Twilio’s name for it: SMS consent is bundled with other communication channels. HighLevel’s rejection guide doesn’t list this code by number, so this page rests on Twilio’s documentation.

Twilio: “It cannot be bundled with consent for other channels.”

twilio.com/docs/api/errors/30556, last updated 27 October 2026, checked 12 November 2026

In plain English

Your form asks for texts together with emails or phone calls in one box or one sentence. Text consent must be its own choice. It applies to both 10DLC campaigns and toll-free number checks.

What usually causes it

  • One checkbox says something like “I agree to receive emails, calls and text messages”.
  • The form has no box that is only for texts.

How to fix it

  1. Split the consent: one box for texts, and separate boxes for email or calls if you need them.
  2. Make the text box unticked and optional, with your business name, STOP and HELP beside it.
  3. Resubmit with a link or screenshot showing the stand-alone text box.

Example

Before and after (change the business name and addresses to yours):

Before: [ ] I agree to receive emails, calls and text messages from Your Business.

After:
[ ] Email me offers and updates.
[ ] Text me about my booking and quotes from Your Business. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

Codes that often come with it

  • 30925: Campaign rejected: Opt-in must be unchecked by default; active consent required
  • 30931: Campaign rejected: Opt-in process must allow consumers to decline
  • 30923: Campaign rejected: Message consent cannot be required for service use

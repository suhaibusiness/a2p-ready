A2P error 30568: what it means and how to fix it

Twilio’s name for it: Call-to-Action is missing the business or brand name. HighLevel’s rejection guide doesn’t list this code by number, so this page rests on Twilio’s documentation.

Twilio: “…does not identify the business or brand that will send them…”

twilio.com/docs/api/errors/30568, last updated 27 October 2026, checked 4 November 2026

In plain English

Your opt-in wording asks people to agree to texts but never says who will text them. Your name in the page header doesn’t count: it has to be in the consent wording itself, and it should match the business name you registered.

What usually causes it

  • The checkbox says “I agree to receive text messages” with no business name.
  • The business name is elsewhere on the page but not in the consent wording.

How to fix it

  1. Put your business name in the consent wording, right where people tick the box.
  2. Use the same name you registered with, so the reviewer can match the two.
  3. Send updated evidence (a screenshot or public link) showing the whole opt-in wording, then resubmit.

Example

Before and after (change the business name and addresses to yours):

Before: I agree to receive text messages.
After: I agree to receive appointment text messages from Your Business. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

Codes that often come with it

  • 30909: Campaign rejected: Message Flow or Call to Action incomplete/unverified
  • 30547: Call-to-Action is missing opt-out instructions
  • 30557: Terms of Service missing business or program name (Twilio)

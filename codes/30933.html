A2P 10DLC rejection code

A2P error 30933: what it means and how to fix it

Twilio’s name for it: Campaign registration rejected: Privacy Policy URL is required for A2P 10DLC campaign. HighLevel’s rejection guide doesn’t list this code by number, so this page rests on Twilio’s documentation.

Twilio: “Error 30933 means the URL field itself was missing from the request.”

twilio.com/docs/api/errors/30933, last updated 27 October 2026, checked 12 November 2026

In plain English

The campaign reached Twilio without a privacy policy address. This isn’t about what your policy says (that’s 30908): the address field was empty or missing. Twilio made the field required for campaigns created through its API from 30 June 2026, so software that never sent it now fails.

What usually causes it

  • The privacy policy address was left blank when the campaign was registered.
  • The software that registers the campaign (your own code or a platform’s) was written before 30 June 2026, when the field was optional, and hasn’t been updated.
  • A reseller’s sign-up never asks its customers for a privacy policy address.
  • The field was sent, but empty.

How to fix it

  1. Publish a privacy policy on your website: public, no login, on an https:// address.
  2. Put that address in the privacy policy field and submit the campaign again.
  3. If you register through a platform and you did fill the field in, send this code to its support: the fix is in its software.
  4. If you write the integration yourself, send PrivacyPolicyUrl with every campaign creation request.

Example

What goes in the field (change the business name and addresses to yours):

Privacy policy URL: https://yourbusiness.com/privacy
(public, https, no login, and the page is a real privacy policy for this business)

Codes that often come with it

  • 30908: Campaign vetting rejection - Compliant Privacy Policy Required

A2P 10DLC rejection code

A2P error 30908: what it means and how to fix it

Twilio’s name for it: Campaign vetting rejection - Compliant Privacy Policy Required. HighLevel’s rejection guide lists the same code.

Twilio: “…did not include the required statement that mobile information and messaging consent are not shared…”

twilio.com/docs/api/errors/30908, last updated 27 October 2026, checked 4 November 2026

In plain English

The reviewer couldn’t confirm your privacy policy is safe for texting. Usually there’s no policy linked, the policy is missing the “we don’t share mobile numbers” statement, or somewhere further down it says you share personal details with partners for marketing.

What usually causes it

  • No privacy policy link in the registration.
  • The policy isn’t on the website you registered, or isn’t linked from the message flow.
  • There are two policies, or the text contradicts itself.
  • A line says you may share or sell personal information or phone numbers to partners or affiliates for marketing.
  • It doesn’t say what you collect and how you use it for texting.
  • For website opt-in: no message frequency, or no “Message and data rates may apply”.

How to fix it

  1. Publish one privacy policy on your own website, public with no login, and link it from every page and from the message flow.
  2. Add the non-sharing statement below, word for word if you like.
  3. Find any sentence about sharing data with partners or affiliates for marketing and delete it or reword it. One such line is enough to fail.
  4. Add a short texting section: what you collect, what the texts are for, “Message frequency varies” and “Message and data rates may apply”.
  5. Remove any second or old policy, then resubmit.

Example

Lines to add to your privacy policy (change the business name and addresses to yours):

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Codes that often come with it

  • 30896: Campaign vetting rejection - Opt-in Error
  • 30919: Campaign rejected: Website lacks sufficient business or messaging use case info
  • 30933: Campaign registration rejected: Privacy Policy URL is required for A2P 10DLC campaign

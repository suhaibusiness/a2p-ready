A2P 10DLC rejection code

A2P error 30911: what it means and how to fix it

Twilio’s name for it: Campaign rejected: Duplicate template text; each field must be unique. HighLevel’s rejection guide lists the same code.

Twilio: “…reuses the same or nearly identical text across multiple registration fields…”

twilio.com/docs/api/errors/30911, last updated 27 October 2026, checked 12 November 2026

In plain English

You pasted the same text (or nearly the same) into more than one box: the description, the message flow and the sample messages. Each box has its own job, and the reviewer needs a different answer in each.

What usually causes it

  • The same paragraph went into the description, the message flow and the samples.
  • The description is generic and doesn’t say who sends the texts, who gets them and why.
  • The message flow repeats the description instead of explaining how people opt in.
  • All sample messages are the same, or don’t look like texts you’d really send.

How to fix it

  1. Description: who sends the texts, who receives them, and why.
  2. Message flow: every way people opt in, with links to the page, your privacy policy and SMS terms.
  3. Samples: two to five different texts you’d really send, each naming your business, with [brackets] around the parts that change, and opt-out wording in at least one.
  4. Read the three boxes side by side; if any two sound alike, rewrite one, then resubmit.

Example

Three boxes, three different jobs (change the business name and addresses to yours):

Description: Your Business, a plumbing company, texts customers who requested a quote or booked a job: booking confirmations, reminders and quote follow-ups.
Message flow: Customers opt in on yourbusiness.com/contact by ticking an optional, unticked SMS box (privacy: yourbusiness.com/privacy, terms: yourbusiness.com/sms-terms).
Sample: Your Business: hi [Name], your quote for [job] is ready: [link]. Reply STOP to opt out.

Codes that often come with it

  • 30909: Campaign rejected: Message Flow or Call to Action incomplete/unverified
  • 30896: Campaign vetting rejection - Opt-in Error

A2P 10DLC rejection code

A2P error 30920: what it means and how to fix it

Twilio’s name for it: Campaign rejected: Website is only a form; insufficient business context. HighLevel’s rejection guide lists the same code.

Twilio: “…does not provide enough public business context for review…”

twilio.com/docs/api/errors/30920, last updated 27 October 2026, checked 12 November 2026

In plain English

The web address you gave is just a form or landing page, so the reviewer couldn’t tell which business is behind the texts. They need a real business website: who you are, what you do, how to contact you, and how people sign up for texts.

What usually causes it

  • The URL is a funnel page, booking page or lead form, not your main website.
  • The page has no business name, description of your services or contact details.
  • The opt-in, privacy policy or terms aren’t publicly reachable from that page.

How to fix it

  1. Give your main business website, not a campaign landing page or form.
  2. Make sure it shows your business name, what you do and how to contact you, and mentions your text messaging.
  3. Link your privacy policy and SMS terms from the page where people opt in.
  4. If pages sit behind a login, give a public link with screenshots of the opt-in instead.
  5. Check the campaign description and sample messages match the business the website shows, then resubmit.

Example

What the submitted page should show (change the business name and addresses to yours):

Your Business: plumbing and hot water repairs in Springfield
Phone, email and address
Contact form with an optional, unticked SMS checkbox and its consent wording
Footer links: Privacy policy, SMS terms

Codes that often come with it

  • 30919: Campaign rejected: Website lacks sufficient business or messaging use case info
  • 30909: Campaign rejected: Message Flow or Call to Action incomplete/unverified
  • 30908: Campaign vetting rejection - Compliant Privacy Policy Required

A2P error 30915: what it means and how to fix it

Twilio’s name for it: Sole Proprietor classification is invalid - business is a registered legal entity. HighLevel’s rejection guide lists the same code.

Twilio: “…the business information provided indicates the business does not align with a sole proprietorship…”

twilio.com/docs/api/errors/30915, last updated 27 October 2026, checked 12 November 2026

In plain English

You registered as a sole proprietor, but the reviewer found a registered business: an LLC, a corporation or another entity, or a tax ID (an EIN, or a Canadian Business Number). Sole proprietor registration is only for US or Canadian businesses with no tax ID. You can resubmit once the details are right.

What usually causes it

  • The brand name includes LLC, Inc. or Corp. instead of your own name.
  • Your website or public records show a registered company.
  • Your tax or registration documents show a company, not a sole proprietorship.
  • The business has an EIN or Canadian Business Number.
  • The details are mixed: a company name with your personal first and last name, or a business type that doesn’t match either.

How to fix it

  1. If you have an LLC, a corporation, a partnership or an EIN, register a Standard or Low-Volume Standard Brand instead, with documents that match the company.
  2. If you really are a sole proprietor, resubmit with your own first and last name and take LLC, Inc. or Corp. out of the brand name.
  3. Make the first name, last name and email all belong to the same person.
  4. Check every business detail matches the others, and your website, before you resubmit.

Example

Which brand type to pick (change the business name and addresses to yours):

No EIN or tax ID, trading as yourself: Sole Proprietor Brand
An LLC, Inc., Corp., partnership, or anything with an EIN: Standard or Low-Volume Standard Brand

A2P 10DLC rejection code

A2P error 30896: what it means and how to fix it

Twilio’s name for it: Campaign vetting rejection - Opt-in Error. HighLevel’s rejection guide lists the same code.

Twilio: “…the opt-in details you provided don't adequately show how end users consent to receive messages…”

twilio.com/docs/api/errors/30896, last updated 27 October 2026, checked 12 November 2026

In plain English

The whole opt-in story didn’t add up for the reviewer. It’s close to 30909, but wider: the message flow, the links you gave, your privacy policy or your sample messages left the reviewer unsure how people agreed to your texts.

What usually causes it

  • The message flow doesn’t say who opts in, where it happens or how consent is collected.
  • You collect numbers in more than one way and only listed some of them.
  • A website, privacy policy or terms link is missing, or the opt-in sits behind a login or on paper with no screenshot.
  • Your privacy policy is missing the line that mobile numbers aren’t shared for marketing, the message frequency, or “Message and data rates may apply”.
  • People can text a keyword to join, but no keywords or opt-in reply were given.
  • The wording suggests the numbers came from somewhere else (bought, shared or collected for another purpose).

How to fix it

  1. Rewrite the message flow: who opts in, where, and how. If there’s more than one way, list every one.
  2. For website opt-in, give the page address plus your privacy policy and terms links. For anything behind a login or on paper, link a public screenshot or photo.
  3. Add the non-sharing line, “Message frequency varies” and “Message and data rates may apply” to your privacy policy.
  4. If people can text a keyword, add the keywords and an opt-in reply with your business name, how to get help and how to opt out.
  5. Make every sample message name your business, and put opt-out wording in at least one.

Example

A sample message that names the business (change the business name and addresses to yours):

Your Business: your appointment is confirmed for Tue 4 Nov at 10am. Reply STOP to opt out.

Codes that often come with it

  • 30909: Campaign rejected: Message Flow or Call to Action incomplete/unverified
  • 30908: Campaign vetting rejection - Compliant Privacy Policy Required
  • 30925: Campaign rejected: Opt-in must be unchecked by default; active consent required

A2P 10DLC rejection code

A2P error 30925: what it means and how to fix it

Twilio’s name for it: Campaign rejected: Opt-in must be unchecked by default; active consent required. HighLevel’s rejection guide lists the same code.

Twilio: “A checkbox or similar consent control cannot be pre-selected by default.”

twilio.com/docs/api/errors/30925, last updated 27 October 2026, checked 12 November 2026

In plain English

Your form opts people in for them, or never asks about texts on its own. A box that’s ticked already doesn’t count, and agreeing to your terms isn’t agreeing to texts. The customer has to tick a texting box themselves.

What usually causes it

  • There’s no separate checkbox for texts.
  • The texting box starts ticked.
  • The only box is “I agree to the terms and privacy policy”.
  • The form isn’t public, and there’s no screenshot showing it.

How to fix it

  1. Change the form itself. Editing the message flow alone won’t clear this code.
  2. Add a checkbox just for texts. It starts unticked, and the form still submits if it’s left empty.
  3. Keep it apart from the terms and privacy box, and from email or phone-call consent.
  4. Put the business name, what the texts are for, frequency, rates, STOP and HELP next to it, with your privacy policy and SMS terms linked below.
  5. If the form is behind a login, put a screenshot on a public page and link it when you resubmit.

Example

The checkbox, in HTML (change the business name and addresses to yours):

<label><input type="checkbox" name="sms_consent"> I agree to receive appointment and quote text messages from Your Business. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.</label>

Codes that often come with it

  • 30909: Campaign rejected: Message Flow or Call to Action incomplete/unverified
  • 30896: Campaign vetting rejection - Opt-in Error
  • 30923: Campaign rejected: Message consent cannot be required for service use

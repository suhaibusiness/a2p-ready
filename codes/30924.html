A2P 10DLC rejection code

A2P error 30924: what it means and how to fix it

Twilio’s name for it: Campaign rejected: Missing or non-compliant consent agreement language in opt-in flow. HighLevel’s rejection guide lists the same code.

Twilio: “…the opt-in flow you provided doesn't contain compliant consent agreement language…”

twilio.com/docs/api/errors/30924, last updated 27 October 2026, checked 12 November 2026

In plain English

The words next to your opt-in box don’t tell people enough before they agree. Carriers want the customer to see, at the moment they give their number, who will text them, what kind of texts, how often, that rates may apply, and how to stop.

What usually causes it

  • The checkbox or form says nothing about texts at all, or only “I agree to be contacted”.
  • No message frequency (for example “Message frequency varies” or “Up to 4 messages a month”).
  • No way to stop: “Reply STOP to opt out” is missing.
  • No “Message and data rates may apply” line.

How to fix it

  1. Put the full consent wording right next to the SMS checkbox: your business name, what the texts are for, how often, “Message and data rates may apply”, “Reply STOP to opt out” and “Reply HELP for help”.
  2. Make the message flow describe that wording at every place people opt in, with links to your privacy policy and SMS terms.
  3. Make sure your privacy policy says mobile numbers aren’t shared with third parties, and repeats the frequency and rates lines.
  4. If the form isn’t public, link a screenshot showing the full wording as customers see it, then resubmit.

Example

Consent wording next to an unticked checkbox (change the business name and addresses to yours):

I agree to receive appointment reminders and quote follow-up texts from Your Business at the number above. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Privacy: yourbusiness.com/privacy. Terms: yourbusiness.com/sms-terms.

Codes that often come with it

  • 30909: Campaign rejected: Message Flow or Call to Action incomplete/unverified
  • 30925: Campaign rejected: Opt-in must be unchecked by default; active consent required
  • 30923: Campaign rejected: Message consent cannot be required for service use
  • 30908: Campaign vetting rejection - Compliant Privacy Policy Required

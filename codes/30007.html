Text delivery error

A2P error 30007: what it means and how to fix it

Twilio’s name for it: Message filtered. This page rests on Twilio’s documentation.

Twilio: “Your message was filtered (blocked) by Twilio or by the carrier.”

twilio.com/docs/api/errors/30007, last updated 11 March 2026, checked 12 November 2026

In plain English

The text was sent, but Twilio’s spam filter or the phone carrier stopped it before it reached the customer. It isn’t a registration rejection: it’s judged message by message, on what the text says and how it’s sent.

What usually causes it

  • Twilio’s filter read the message as spam or unwanted messaging.
  • A wireless carrier flagged the message as objectionable and blocked it.
  • The message or the way you text breaks Twilio’s Messaging Policy or Acceptable Use Policy, or a carrier’s rules.

How to fix it

  1. Read your message against Twilio’s Messaging Policy and Acceptable Use Policy, and text only people who agreed to hear from you.
  2. Make every text say who it’s from and match what people signed up for (the use case you registered).
  3. Read Twilio’s help articles on how message filtering works and how to keep messages from being filtered (linked from Twilio’s 30007 page).
  4. If you’re sure the texts are compliant, collect 3 or more Message SIDs showing “undelivered” with 30007 and open a ticket with Twilio Support so they can review them.

Example

A text that says who it’s from and why (change the business name and addresses to yours):

Your Business: hi [Name], thanks for your quote request. Your quote for [job] is ready: [link]. Reply STOP to opt out.

Codes that often come with it

  • 30034: US A2P 10DLC - Message from an Unregistered Number

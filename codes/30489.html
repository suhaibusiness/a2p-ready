Toll-free verification rejection

A2P error 30489: what it means and how to fix it

Twilio’s name for it: Toll Free verification rejection - Website must be established and active. This page rests on Twilio’s documentation.

Twilio: “…Submit a fully developed, live website with clear business information…”

twilio.com/docs/api/errors/30489, last updated 11 March 2026, checked 12 November 2026

In plain English

Your toll-free number was refused because the reviewer couldn’t see a real, working business behind it. The website looked unfinished, not launched, or too thin to show who you are and what you sell. Texts from that number stay blocked until it’s verified.

What usually causes it

  • The site is under construction, a holding page, or not live yet.
  • There’s no address, phone number or email to contact the business.
  • The site doesn’t say what services you offer.
  • The business name or details on the site don’t match what you submitted.

How to fix it

  1. Finish the site before you resubmit: a home page that says what you do, a services page, and a contact page.
  2. Show your contact details: phone, email and business address or service area.
  3. Use the same business name, website and details on the site as in the verification form.
  4. Keep the privacy policy and SMS terms linked from the site, since reviewers look at them too.
  5. Resubmit the verification once the live site shows all of this.

Example

The least a site needs before you resubmit (change the business name and addresses to yours):

Home: Your Business, licensed plumber in [area], what you do in one line.
Services: the jobs you take, in plain words.
Contact: phone, email, business address or service area, opening hours.
Footer: Privacy Policy · SMS Terms.

Codes that often come with it

  • 30919: Campaign rejected: Website lacks sufficient business or messaging use case info
  • 30556: SMS consent is bundled with other communication channels

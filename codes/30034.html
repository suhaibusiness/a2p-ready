A2P 10DLC sending error

A2P error 30034: what it means and how to fix it

Twilio’s name for it: US A2P 10DLC - Message from an Unregistered Number. This page rests on Twilio’s documentation.

Twilio: “…Twilio blocks messages to U.S. numbers when you send from a +1 10DLC number…”

twilio.com/docs/api/errors/30034, last updated 21 April 2026, checked 12 November 2026

In plain English

Your texts to US numbers aren’t going out, because the number you send from isn’t attached to an approved A2P 10DLC campaign. It isn’t a rejection of your campaign: the number isn’t registered yet, or isn’t linked to the campaign that was approved. Every text from that number to a US phone is blocked until it is.

What usually causes it

  • Your brand or campaign registration isn’t finished or approved yet, so the number still counts as unregistered.
  • The campaign is approved, but this number isn’t in the sender pool of the Messaging Service linked to that campaign.
  • You bought or ported a new number and never added it to the approved campaign’s Messaging Service.
  • You put more than one number on a Sole Proprietor campaign. Only one number can be registered on that type; the others stay unregistered.

How to fix it

  1. Check where your brand and campaign stand. If either is still pending or was rejected, finish or fix that first: nothing else clears 30034.
  2. Once the campaign is approved, add the sending number to the sender pool of the Messaging Service linked to it.
  3. Check the number’s A2P status again. If it still shows unregistered, fix the link between the Messaging Service and the approved campaign.
  4. On a Sole Proprietor campaign, keep only the one number you want to text from in that Messaging Service.
  5. If you must text before registration is done, Twilio’s alternatives are a toll-free number, a short code, or Twilio Verify for login codes.

Example

A quick check, top to bottom (change the business name and addresses to yours):

1. Brand: approved?
2. Campaign: approved (not pending, not rejected)?
3. Messaging Service linked to that campaign: is my sending number in its sender pool?
4. Sole Proprietor campaign: only one number in that pool?
All four yes: send a test text to your own US number.

Codes that often come with it

  • 30909: Campaign rejected: Message Flow or Call to Action incomplete/unverified
  • 30915: Sole Proprietor classification is invalid - business is a registered legal entity

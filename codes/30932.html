A2P 10DLC rejection code

A2P error 30932: what it means and how to fix it

Twilio’s name for it: Campaign rejected: Privacy policy must disclose third-party data sharing. HighLevel’s rejection guide lists the same code.

Twilio: “…Remove or revise any privacy policy language that suggests SMS consent can be shared…”

twilio.com/docs/api/errors/30932, last updated 27 October 2026, checked 12 November 2026

In plain English

Your privacy policy either says customer details may be shared with partners, affiliates or other companies, or never says that mobile numbers and texting consent are kept to yourself. Reviewers need that promise in writing, on a public page.

What usually causes it

  • A line in the policy allows sharing, selling or passing on customer information, and doesn’t exclude mobile numbers.
  • The policy has no clear sentence saying mobile numbers and opt-in data won’t be shared for marketing.
  • The policy isn’t public, or the message flow doesn’t link to it.
  • Consent is collected once and passed to partner businesses, instead of each business collecting its own.

How to fix it

  1. Add the non-sharing sentence below to your privacy policy, word for word or close to it.
  2. Remove or reword any line that lets mobile numbers or texting consent be shared, sold, rented or passed on.
  3. Publish the policy on your website and link it in the message flow when you resubmit.
  4. Show the same privacy link next to every place you collect phone numbers for texts.
  5. If more than one business texts customers, each one collects its own consent.

Example

The sentence reviewers look for (change the business name and addresses to yours):

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with, sold or rented to any third party.

Codes that often come with it

  • 30908: Campaign vetting rejection - Compliant Privacy Policy Required
  • 30933: Campaign registration rejected: Privacy Policy URL is required for A2P 10DLC campaign
  • 30909: Campaign rejected: Message Flow or Call to Action incomplete/unverified

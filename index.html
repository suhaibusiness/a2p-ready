Where the rules come from

Every check is tied to a source we read: Twilio’s A2P 10DLC error pages (including the more detailed codes from March 2026), Twilio’s registration guide, the CTIA Messaging Principles and HighLevel’s A2P help articles. Each result links to the Twilio code it can cause, so you can read it yourself.

The pages we write come from fixed templates filled with your details, not from AI, so the wording is the same every time.

Questions

What is A2P 10DLC?

It’s the US carriers’ registration for businesses that text customers from an ordinary 10-digit number. You register your business (the brand) and what you’ll send (the campaign). Unregistered texts get blocked.

Does it cost more to resubmit after a rejection?

Twilio and HighLevel both say the campaign vetting fee is charged once per campaign, so fixing and resubmitting the same campaign doesn’t cost another fee. Creating a new campaign does.

How long does approval take?

Twilio says standard campaigns can take up to 5 business days to review. Sole proprietor campaigns are usually quicker.

I don’t have a website. Can I still register?

Carriers check your website, so you need one. Step 3 builds a small 3-page website (home page, privacy policy and SMS terms), with your contact details and an opt-in form on the home page. You can host it anywhere or paste it into a HighLevel funnel.

I already got rejected. Where do I start?

Open the rejection helper, paste the message or error code, and it tells you what it means and which fix in the kit covers it.

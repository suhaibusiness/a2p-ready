Privacy

Last updated 3 November 2026. In short: nothing you type into A2P Ready is sent to us. It stays in your browser.

What stays in your browser

  • Your business details, your registration answers and your check results are saved in this browser’s local storage (keys a2p-state and a2p-theme), so the tool remembers them next time. They never leave your device unless you save a project file or a download yourself, and those go to your own computer.
  • Website pages you paste into the check are read in your browser and then dropped. Only the results are kept, never the pages themselves.
  • The privacy policy, SMS terms, opt-in form and website the tool builds are made in your browser from fixed templates. No AI service sees your details.

What leaves your browser

  • Nothing you enter. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no cookies.
  • This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives the normal request details when you load a page, and GitHub says it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. See GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • Links to Twilio’s error pages and other sources open those sites only when you click them.

Deleting your details

Clear this site’s data in your browser settings (site data or cookies for this address). That removes everything the tool saved. Project files you downloaded are yours to keep or delete.

Not legal advice

A2P Ready explains carrier and Twilio rules in plain English and builds pages from templates. It is not legal advice, and it can’t promise approval: the carriers decide.

Who runs this

A2P Ready is a free beta made by Suhaib Akmal. It is independent and not part of Twilio, HighLevel or any carrier.
